Who we are

About.

We build gardens that feel like a natural part of the home, made for the way you actually live outside.

Creative Green is a small Adelaide studio working across the city and the Hills. We believe a garden is worth doing slowly and doing once. That means honest materials, levels that work with the land rather than against it, and planting chosen to look better in ten years than it does on handover day.
